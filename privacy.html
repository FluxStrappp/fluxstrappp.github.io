Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FluxStrap handles user data.

Roblox account information

FluxStrap does not collect Roblox passwords, Roblox cookies, account tokens, or personal account information.

Remote update checks

FluxStrap may connect to a remote system only to check update information, version notices, announcements, and official download information.

Data sharing

FluxStrap does not sell or share user data.
