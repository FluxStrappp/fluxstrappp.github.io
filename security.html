FluxStrap Security

FluxStrap is a Roblox performance launcher. This page explains what it does and what it does not do.

What FluxStrap does not collect

  • FluxStrap does not ask for your Roblox password.
  • FluxStrap does not collect Roblox cookies.
  • FluxStrap does not collect Roblox account tokens.
  • FluxStrap does not collect personal Roblox account information.

Not a cheat or exploit

FluxStrap is not a cheat, exploit, injector, bypass tool, or Robux generator. It is designed for performance profiles, graphics settings, FastFlags, favorite game profiles, Roblox repair tools, and launcher customization.
